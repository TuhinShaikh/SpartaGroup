## Gen AI

AI that creates new text, images, or code rather than just sorting or predicting existing data.

It learns patterns from huge amounts of existing content, then generates new content that fits those patterns.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

## LLMs

**LLM** stands for **Large Language Model**.

It is trained on massive amounts of text to predict “what word comes next”. At huge scale, that simple mechanism produces surprisingly coherent, useful answers.

</td>
</tr>

## Gen AI Use Cases

- **Writing:** emails and other drafts
- **Coding:** writing, explaining, and debugging code
- **Customer service:** chatbots and support agents
- **Data analysis:** turning spreadsheets into plain-English insights

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

## AI in Everyday Life

- Meal planning or recipe ideas based on what is in the fridge
- Drafting a tricky text message or email reply
- Trip planning, itineraries, and restaurant picks
- Fantasy Football team making
- Troubleshooting a random tech or household problem

## The Four Properties of AI

| Property | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Next-token prediction** | Predicting what should come next in a sequence |
| **Knowledge** | Information learned from training data |
| **Working memory** | Using the current conversation or task as context |
| **Steerability** | Following instructions and adapting its output |
